01The problem

Most great island ideas stall before the first device is placed.

Not for lack of imagination. The gap between “I have an idea” and “I know what to build first” is where most projects stop.

a.

The idea is still vague

“Something like a tycoon, but with fighting” is a feeling, not a design. It needs a defined game type before it can become a build.

b.

The toolbox is wide

UEFN offers a large catalogue of devices. Knowing which handful your game actually needs takes research most people never get to.

c.

There is no order of work

An empty editor gives no hint of what to do first, second or last. Without a sequence, momentum fades.

02The promise

An idea goes in. A buildable plan comes out.

Forge does the narrowing for you. It matches your idea to a researched game type, picks the devices that game relies on, and orders the work into five steps you can follow in UEFN.

  • A defined game type, not a loose genre
  • Only the UEFN devices your game needs
  • Five build steps, in the order you do them

07Questions

Good questions, short answers.

Do I need to know UEFN already?

No. The plan tells you which devices to use and in what order, so it works as a guide for your first island as well as a shortcut for your tenth.

What exactly do I get at the end?

Three things: the exact game type your idea fits, the list of UEFN devices that game needs, and five build steps to follow in the editor.

Which ways to start are available now?

Answering questions is ready today. Spin the wheel and Chat with AI are on the way.

What if my idea doesn't fit a game type?

Forge narrows your answers to the closest of its 13 researched game types, then builds the plan around it. You can start again and steer it in a different direction at any time.

Does Forge build the island for me?

Forge designs it. You build it in UEFN, with AI tools to help along the way. The plan is what makes that build quick and focused.
